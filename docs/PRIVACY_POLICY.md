# Privacy Policy for Galactic Merge: Space Tycoon

**Last updated:** September 09, 2026

**Sarankar Developers** ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile application **Galactic Merge: Space Tycoon** (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
- **Gameplay Progress & State**: Starship fleet tiers, credits, Dark Matter, unlocked tracks, achievements, and game preferences are stored locally on your device using local storage (`SharedPreferences`). This data never leaves your device unless backed up through your standard Google Play / Apple iCloud device backups.

### B. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services
We do not directly collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your real name, physical address, or phone number). However, the App integrates third-party SDKs that may collect information used to identify your device:

1. **Google AdMob (Google LLC)**:
   - Used for serving banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements.
   - May collect: Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), IP address, general location data, device make/model, and ad interaction statistics.
   - Privacy Policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
   - Ad Technologies: [https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

2. **Google Play Billing & Apple In-App Purchases (StoreKit)**:
   - Used for processing purchases of VIP Passes, Dark Matter packs, and Credit bundles.
   - All financial transactions are handled securely by Google Play (Google LLC) or Apple App Store (Apple Inc.). We never collect or have access to your credit card, bank details, or billing address.
   - Google Payments Privacy: [https://payments.google.com/legaldocument?family=privacy_policy](https://payments.google.com/legaldocument?family=privacy_policy)
   - Apple Privacy Policy: [https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## 2. User Consent and Privacy Frameworks (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

### General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — European Economic Area (EEA) & UK
If you are located in the EEA or UK, we utilize the **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)** SDK to request your consent prior to serving personalized advertisements. You may update or revoke your consent at any time via the in-game Settings modal.

### California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
If you are a California resident, you have the right to request information about the categories of personal data collected by advertising partners. We do not sell your personal information.

---

## 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
Our App is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided personal information without parental consent, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.

---

## 4. Security of Data
The security of your data is important to us. Gameplay state is maintained on your local device. Third-party partners utilize industry-standard encryption and security protocols to safeguard advertising identifiers and transactional telemetry.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top.
