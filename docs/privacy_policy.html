Privacy Policy for Galactic Merge: Space Tycoon

Last Updated: September 09, 2026

Sarankar Developers ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile game Galactic Merge: Space Tycoon (the "App"). This page explains how your information is handled when you use our App and the privacy rights you have.

1. Information We Collect & Store

A. Local Gameplay Data

Your game progress (fleet tiers, credits, Dark Matter, upgrades, achievements, and settings) is stored locally on your device storage (SharedPreferences). We do not transmit or store this data on our own servers.

B. Automatically Collected Data by Third-Party SDKs

We do not collect or request personally identifiable information (such as your real name, physical address, or phone number). However, our App integrates verified third-party partners for monetization and billing:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Serves banner, rewarded, and interstitial ads. AdMob may collect anonymous device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), IP address, and general telemetry.
    Google Privacy Policy | Google Ads Technologies
  • Google Play In-App Billing & Apple StoreKit: Handles purchases for VIP Passes and virtual currency bundles. All payment processing is managed by Google Play or Apple. We never receive or store your financial or payment details.

2. User Consent (GDPR, UK & CCPA)

For players in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, we integrate the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain your explicit consent before serving personalized advertisements. You can modify or revoke your consent preferences at any time in the in-game Settings.

3. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our App is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

4. Data Security

We prioritize the security of your data. Local game files are stored within your device's sandboxed environment, and all third-party services employ industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS).

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
